Declutter Privacy Policy

Effective October 17, 2026

The short version

Declutter does not collect, store, or share any of your data. It has no accounts, ads, analytics, crash reporting, or tracking, and it works fully offline.

Your photos

Declutter reads your photo library only on your device, with the access you grant in iOS. Photos and videos are never uploaded or sent anywhere by Declutter. You can change or remove access at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Photos.

What stays on your device

To remember your progress, Declutter saves which items you kept or queued for deletion, your settings, and on-device analysis used to find similar photos. This information stays inside the app on your device and is removed when you delete the app. Reset Progress in Settings clears your review history.

Deleting photos

Items you delete are moved to the Recently Deleted album in Photos, where you can recover them for 30 days. If iCloud Photos is on, iOS also removes them from your other devices.

iCloud downloads

If you turn on Download from iCloud, iOS may download full-size originals of photos stored in iCloud. That transfer is handled by Apple's Photos framework, between your device and iCloud. Declutter receives no data from it beyond the photo itself.

Purchases

Declutter Pro is purchased through the App Store. Apple processes the payment, and Declutter only learns whether Pro is unlocked. Apple's handling of purchase data is covered by Apple's privacy policy.

Children

Declutter collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted with the app and on its App Store page. For questions, use the support link on Declutter's App Store page.
