Declutter Support

Declutter helps you clear out your camera roll: swipe left to delete, right to keep, then delete everything you queued in one step.

Where do deleted photos go?

To the Recently Deleted album in Photos. You can recover them there for 30 days. Space is freed once you empty Recently Deleted, or after 30 days.

Does deleting remove photos from iCloud?

Yes. If iCloud Photos is on, deleting a photo removes it from all your devices.

Why can't some items be deleted?

Items in shared albums or synced from a computer can't be deleted by apps, so Declutter skips them.

Restoring Declutter Pro

Pro is a one-time purchase. On a new device, open Settings in Declutter and tap Restore Purchases while signed in to the same Apple Account.
